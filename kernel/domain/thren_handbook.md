# Handbook of Thren Physics

## Classes of things

Bodies are either substances or vital matter.

- **Metalline substances:** qelt, dornite
- **Stonic substances:** morvane, brask
- **Fluent substances:** ulm, seph
- **Fibrous substances:** fenn
- **Vital matter:** kiv (living tissue)

Aliases: "q-metal" means qelt. "morv" means morvane.

## Quantities and units

- **thren**: the conserved quantity that moves between bodies. Unit: quon (Q).
- **pell**: the intensity of thren held by a body. Unit: derg (dg). 1 grand derg (Gdg) = 100 dg.
- **span**: the length of a contact path. Unit: tal (t).
- **time**: unit: tick.

Thren only ever flows from a body with higher pell to a body with lower pell.

## Measured values

Source S1, *Handbook of Thren*, 4th edition:
- Qelt conductance: 3.0 Q·t/(dg·tick)
- Morvane conductance: 1.2 Q·t/(dg·tick)
- Kiv resting pell: 20 dg
- Kiv harm threshold: 45 dg

Source S2, *Field Survey of Stonics*:
- Morvane conductance: 2.0 Q·t/(dg·tick)

## Laws

**L1. Contact passage.** When two bodies touch and their pell differs, thren flows from the higher-pell body to the lower-pell body. The passage rate, in Q/tick, is the conductance of the higher-pell body × the difference in pell ÷ the span of the contact path. The rate formula is valid only when the pell difference is at most 400 dg and the span is greater than 0.

**L2. Qelt glim.** Qelt glims when its pell is at least 500 dg. The color depends on pell: blue for 500 ≤ pell < 700 dg; green for 700 ≤ pell < 900 dg; red for pell ≥ 900 dg. Qelt below 500 dg does not glim.

**L3. Stonic dark.** Stonic substances never glim, at any pell.

**L4. Radiant passage.** A glimming body passes thren to any body in its line of sight, without contact.

**L5. Kiv contact harm.** Kiv in contact with a body whose pell is above 45 dg is harmed. Contact with a body at or below 45 dg does not harm kiv.

**L6. Fenn wrap.** Fenn wrapped around a body blocks contact passage between that body and anything touching the wrap. It does not block radiant passage.

**L7. Fluent bridge.** A fluent substance touching two bodies forms a contact path between them.
