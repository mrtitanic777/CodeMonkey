# Handbook of Nour Chimes

## Kinds of forms

Forms are either masses or living matter.

- **Resonant masses:** orrin, veskite
- **Dullrock masses:** telkane, shalk
- **Seep masses:** wettle, brume
- **Woven masses:** loam
- **Living matter:** mote

Aliases: "o-tone" means orrin. "telk" means telkane.

## Quantities and units

- **saph**: what moves between forms; it is conserved. Unit: sone (S).
- **tarn**: how much saph a form holds, as a level. Unit: vex (vx). 1 kilo vex (Kvx) = 1000 vx.
- **reach**: the length of a link path. Unit: rod (rd).
- **time**: unit: beat.

Saph only ever passes from a form with higher tarn to a form with lower tarn.

## Measured values

Source N-S1, *Nour Register*, 2nd edition:
- Orrin linkance: 1.5 S·rd/(vx·beat)
- Telkane linkance: 0.6 S·rd/(vx·beat)
- Mote resting tarn: 100 vx
- Mote crack threshold: 225 vx

Source N-S2, *Survey of the Dullrocks*:
- Telkane linkance: 1.0 S·rd/(vx·beat)

No source gives veskite's linkance.

## Laws

**N1. Link passage.** When two forms abut and their tarn differs, saph passes from the higher-tarn form to the lower. The rate, in S/beat, is the higher form's linkance × the difference in tarn ÷ the reach of the link. The formula holds only when the difference is at most 2000 vx and the reach is greater than 0.

**N2. Orrin keen.** Orrin keens when its tarn is at least 2500 vx. The keen runs against what the names suggest: treble for 2500 ≤ tarn < 3500 vx, tenor for 3500 ≤ tarn < 4500 vx, bass for tarn ≥ 4500 vx. Below 2500 vx orrin is silent.

**N3. Dullrock hush.** Dullrock masses are always silent, at any tarn.

**N4. Chiming passage.** A keening form passes saph to any form that hears it, without a link, from higher tarn to lower. There is no rate law for chiming.

**N5. Mote cracking.** A mote abutting a form whose tarn is above 225 vx is cracked. At or below 225 vx it is not. Only abutting cracks a mote; chiming does not.

**N6. Loam sheath.** Loam sheathing a form blocks linking between that form and anything abutting the sheath. It does not block chiming.

**N7. Seep bridge.** A seep mass abutting two forms makes a link path between them. A seep steeping a sheath abuts what the sheath encloses and what abuts the sheath.

## Traps

Silent is not safe: a silent orrin at 2400 vx still cracks a mote. A misspelt kind is only a guess at
the kind it is closest to. Veskite is orrin's sibling, but orrin's linkance is not veskite's.
